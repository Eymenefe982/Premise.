Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Premise is a literature search tool for clinicians and researchers. Because the questions you ask can reveal clinical context about a patient, we treat everything you type into the search box as sensitive. This page explains what we collect, where it goes, and who can see it.

1. Your search content is private to your account

Every search, its result, and your library are stored under your account only. There is no admin screen, support tool, or API endpoint in Premise that lets anyone (including the people who run this service) open another account's questions, results, or library from inside the application. Administrative access is limited to account status (plan, credit balance, email verification) and never extends to search content.

Shared answer cache. To avoid paying twice for the same work, the answer to a question is kept for up to 7 days. If another user asks the identical question with the same settings in that window, they receive the same literature summary, marked "reused", without learning who asked it or when beyond the age of the answer. Each cached answer is linked to the account whose question produced it and is deleted together with that account; otherwise it is deleted automatically after 7 days.

What this does not cover: whoever operates the underlying database server (currently Neon/Postgres) retains the technical ability to query it directly, the way any database administrator can. Premise does not build a screen or tool for that, and does not use search content for any purpose beyond producing your answer. If you need data fully unreadable even at the infrastructure level, do not include patient-identifying details in your questions; ask about the clinical scenario in de-identified form.

2. Who else your question is sent to

To produce an answer, parts of your question and the matched literature are sent to:

RecipientWhat it receivesWhy
Google (Gemini API) Your question (translated), and abstracts/excerpts of the matched articles Generates the summarised answer
PubMed / PubMed Central / Europe PMC (NCBI and partners) Search terms derived from your question Finds matching articles. These are public literature databases, not your identity
OpenAlex and Unpaywall Identifiers (DOIs) of the matched articles, never your question Adds citation counts and links to legal free full texts
Brevo (email delivery) Your email address, for account emails only (welcome, verification, password reset, account deletion) Delivers transactional email, never your search content

None of these recipients receive your name, and your account email is never attached to the text sent to Google or to PubMed/Europe PMC. Your email goes only to Brevo, solely to deliver account email.

3. Where data is stored

Accounts, credit balances, search history and library entries are stored in a managed Postgres database (Neon). Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. Sign-in sessions are signed tokens kept in your browser's cookies; the server does not store them, only a fingerprint of a session you ended by signing out, so it cannot be reused.

For security, sign-in events (sign-in, sign-out, password changes, failed attempts, account deletion) are written to the server log with the time, your IP address and a masked form of your email address (for example a***@example.com). These log lines never contain your questions or results. They are kept by our hosting provider (Render) for a limited period and then rotated out.

4. Special-category data and cross-border transfer

Clinical questions can qualify as special-category personal data. Sending them to Google's Gemini API and storing them on Neon means data leaves Türkiye during processing. We ask for your explicit, informed consent to this at signup (see the consent checkbox on the sign-up form). You can withdraw consent at any time by closing your account; see section 6.

Cookies

Both are HTTP-only (not readable by scripts) and sent only over HTTPS. We do not use advertising, tracking or third-party cookies. If usage statistics are enabled, they are collected with a cookieless, privacy-friendly counter that does not identify you or follow you across sites.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page opens, your browser requests the font files from Google, which receives your IP address and browser details as part of that request. No cookies are set and none of your account or search content is included.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not use your searches to train models.
  • We do not attach your identity to the text sent for literature search or summarisation.

6. Your rights

You can delete your account yourself at any time from your account page (My account → Delete account). Deletion is immediate and permanent: it removes your account, search history and reports, library, credit history, and the cached answers created from your questions. Only the masked security log lines described in section 3 remain until the hosting provider rotates them out.

Deleting your account also withdraws your consent. To request a copy of your data, or if you cannot sign in, contact us at the address below.

7. Not a clinical decision tool

Premise summarises published literature. It does not diagnose, does not recommend treatment, and is not a substitute for clinical judgement. Every generated summary must be verified against the cited articles before it is used in a clinical decision. Do not enter identifying patient information (name, MRN, exact birth date, etc.) into the search box; describe the clinical question in de-identified form.
